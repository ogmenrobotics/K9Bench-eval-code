Human Evaluation Guidelines

1. Watch the Video
  • Play the video fully before answering (auto-plays, muted, no controls).
  • Observe the dog’s posture, tail position, body movement, interaction, and surrounding context.
  • Replay the video if necessary to confirm details.
2. Review the Question
  • Ensure the question is directly relevant to the video content.
  • Use appropriate canine action terms, posture cues, interaction descriptions, and clear cause–effect relationships in the response.
  • Clearly trace interactions and behavioral changes when applicable.
3. Provide an Open-Ended Answer
  • Write a clear, factual description strictly based on visible evidence in the video.
  • Do not assume intent, emotion, or context that is not visually or audibly observable.
