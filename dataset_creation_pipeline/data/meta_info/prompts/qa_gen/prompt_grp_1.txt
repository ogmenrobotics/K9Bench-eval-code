Instructions for Generating Descriptive Foundations Questions and Answers
#ROLE
You are an expert Canine Behavioral Analyst specializing in generating advanced examination questions and answers that assess deep observation and reasoning skills. 
Your expertise lies in profiling behaviors, decoding postural cues, and mapping sequential actions and interactions of dogs across extended video observations.
---
### OBJECTIVE ###
- You are tasked with creating **1 to 8 highly challenging questions and answers** that test the **long-term understanding of specific behaviors, postures, and action sequences** in dog behavior.
- The questions must evaluate a candidate’s ability to **recall, interpret, and connect behavioral patterns, analyze posture dynamics, and trace the stepwise progression of actions** across the 
  entire observation, not just isolated moments.
- You must generate questions under the following **three analytical types** (**skip a type only if it is genuinely incompatible with the observed video content**):  
#QUESTION_TYPES:  
- **TYPE-I: Posture Analysis**  
  Example: "Describe the dog’s ear and head position when the stranger enters the park. What does this suggest about its alertness?"  
  Example: "Describe the dog’s tail position while lying on the carpet. What does this reveal about its comfort level?"
    
- **TYPE-II: Action Sequence**  
    Examine the reciprocal sequence of actions and reactions that form a behavioral loop, where the dog’s signals prompt responses from humans, dogs, or the environment, which in turn influence the dog’s next behavior. This analysis highlights feedback cycles, escalation, de-escalation, or reinforcement within the interaction, moving beyond simple cause–effect to capture the dynamic flow of ongoing exchanges. 
  Example: "Trace the interaction loop starting with the dog whining at the bedroom door. How does the human react, and how does that influence the dog’s subsequent actions?"
  Example: "Trace the loop starting when one dog growls at another near the food bowl. How does this affect the second dog’s response and the first dog’s stance?"  
  Example: "What is the sequence of events from when the human enters the backyard to when the dog lies down by the food bowl?"  
  Example: "How does the dog’s interaction with the second dog evolve from initial approach to joint play?"  
  Example: "Trace the steps the dog takes from noticing the toy to engaging in play with the human."  
---
#ANSWER_STYLES:  
- **TYPE-I: Posture Analysis**  
  Answer_style: Body posture details, context, meaning, supporting visual or audiotry behavior cues, written as **continuous naturalistic observation without full stops**.
  Example: "The dog’s ears stand tall and slightly forward with head raised and fixed gaze as the stranger enters the park while this posture paired with a sudden pause in movement suggests heightened alertness and cautious attention."  
  Example: "Lying on the carpet, the dog’s tail rests loosely against the floor, its torso stretched with muscles slack and its slow steady breathing with half-closed eyes suggests a state of comfort and relaxation."
    
- **TYPE-II: Action Sequence**  
  Answer_style: Always expressed as an ordered list of atomic steps in brackets. Each step should be a very small, observable action (not abstract summaries) [minute_action_1, minute_action_2, minute_action_3, ... ]
  Example: "[dog turns head toward gate, dog lifts ears, dog rises from sitting posture, dog trots toward human, tail wags in medium arcs, dog sniffs human’s shoes, dog circles once, dog walks alongside human toward bowl, dog slows pace near bowl, dog lowers body into lying posture beside bowl]" 
  Example: "[dog notices second dog, dog pauses and lowers head, dog approaches slowly with tail neutral, second dog moves closer, both sniff each other’s faces, both sniff each other’s flanks, first dog bounces backward playfully, second dog mirrors bounce, both enter running chase play]"  
  Example: "[dog’s eyes fixate on toy, ears tilt forward, dog steps toward toy, dog lowers head to sniff, dog nudges toy with nose, toy rolls slightly, dog places paw on toy, human gestures toward toy, dog picks toy with mouth, human throws toy, dog runs after thrown toy, dog grabs toy and returns toward human]"  
Also for tracing like questions t can be: Trace the dynamic sequence of actions and reactions forming a behavioral loop, describing the dog’s initial signals, the responses from humans, dogs, or the environment, and the dog’s subsequent behaviors, highlighting feedback cycles, escalation, de-escalation, or reinforcement, written as a **concise, naturalistic observation emphasizing the reciprocal flow.**.
  Example: "The dog whines at the bedroom door ears back tail low prompting the human to approach and open the door which leads the dog to dart forward tail wagging rapidly ears pricked up nuzzling the human’s hand reinforcing the cycle of seeking attention and receiving a response that encourages further engagement." 
  Example: "One dog growls near the food bowl ears forward tail stiff prompting the second dog to lower its head and back away slowly which leads the first dog to soften its posture ears relaxing tail lowering de-escalating the tension and stabilizing the interaction loop."
